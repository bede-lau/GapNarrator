# GapNarrator Product Context

register: product

## Product Purpose

GapNarrator is a timed audio-description production tool for accessible video. It helps creators find silent gaps, compress narration, and preview whether an audio-description track fits without covering dialogue.

## Users

- Accessibility producers preparing audio-description tracks.
- Hackathon judges evaluating whether the timing problem is obvious.
- Video creators who need a fast proof that narration can fit into dialogue gaps.

## Tone

Calm, precise, editorial, and production-grade. The UI should feel like a native Apple pro utility, not a generic AI dashboard or a scene-description chatbot.

## Anti-References

- Generic AI video analyzer.
- Decorative dark dashboard with glowing cards.
- Chat-first upload tool.
- Overly old-film themed interface.

## Strategic Principles

- The timeline and timing score are the product.
- Video analysis is a means to create a timed audio-description track.
- Fallback behavior must remain demo-ready without OpenAI.
